Getting started

Ready in a minute

  1. Save your phrases

    Write the lines you use most and choose a folder for each.

  2. Add the keyboard

    Go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Add New Keyboard, choose Paste Keyboard and turn on Allow Full Access.

  3. Switch with the globe key

    In any app, tap the globe key to switch to Paste Keyboard, then tap a phrase.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, download it and start right away. Premium is available if you want more folders and unlimited saves.

Why does it need Full Access?

Full Access lets the keyboard load the phrases you saved in the app and paste them. Paste Keyboard has no letter keys, so it never receives what you type in other apps.

Will I lose my phrases if I switch phones?

Back up with your Google account and sign in on the new device to restore them. You can also move them with a file.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15.0 or later on the App Store, and Android phones on Google Play.

Which languages does it support?

English, Korean, Japanese, Thai and Spanish.
